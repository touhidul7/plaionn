# PLAIONN progress preview

Open the preview link provided with your login details. Sign in, go to **My account**, and open **PLAIONN Client Demo**. Use fictional data while testing.

## Suggested walkthrough (15–20 minutes)

| Area | What to try | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| League HQ | Open Season settings; review roster limits, game periods, points, fees, and tiebreaker order. Change one setting and save. | Your setting remains after refresh. Fee values are configuration; no charge is made. |
| Teams | Open Northline HC > Manage roster. Change a player's jersey or position; mark a player inactive and reactivate them. | Changes persist. Inactive records retain history. Duplicate active jersey numbers are rejected. |
| Schedule | Open the demo game; change its time with a reason. Try creating another game for the same teams at an overlapping time. | Rescheduling saves; overlapping team/rink bookings are rejected. |
| Officials | If official test accounts were supplied, assign a timekeeper and referees. | Assignments appear, and overlapping bookings for the same official are rejected. |
| Scorekeeping | Open the scheduled demo game. Record a home goal at period 1, 10:00 remaining, with an assist. Add an away penalty. | The goal changes the provisional score; both events appear in the log and persist after refresh. |
| Corrections | As commissioner, void the goal with a reason. | The score reverses; the original event remains under Voided events. |
| Registration | In a private window, create/sign in to a separate player account. Go to My account > Apply for a season and apply to Client Review Season. | The application shows Pending. |
| Review | In the commissioner window, open League HQ > Manage registration and approve the player. | The applicant sees Approved and can open the league from My account after refreshing. Team placement is handled separately through the roster. |
| Mobile | Repeat a roster or schedule step on your phone. | Navigation and forms remain usable; wide tables scroll sideways. |

The commissioner account can test scoring and corrections without creating additional official accounts. If you receive role-specific accounts: the timekeeper records events; referees review and void events; players do not have scoring access. Use separate browser sessions to compare roles.

Scores in this preview are provisional. Refresh another session to see saved updates. Waivers, payments, automatic live updates, offline scoring, approved results/standings, and video highlights are upcoming work.

To report feedback, send the page, what you tried, what you expected, and a screenshot if useful.
